# PRODUCT TRUTH: BOOKCHAIN LIBRARY

## Vision
**"LIBRARY FIRST, COPYRIGHT SECOND, BLOCKCHAIN UNDERNEATH."**

A premium digital reading room that doubles as an immutable copyright registry. It is designed primarily as an academic and cultural archive. The blockchain mechanics operate silently in the background, only surfacing when an author needs to register or a reader needs to verify a work.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Discover & Read**: A serene, distraction-free environment to browse and read digital works.
2. **Verify Copyright**: Any user can verify the cryptographic hash of a file against the BookChain registry.
3. **Register Copyright**: Authors can claim and register their works immutably using a connected wallet.

## Global Capabilities (New)
4. **Bilingual System**: Supports English (Default) and Vietnamese (`EN | VI`), preserving state via localStorage. Fully localized UI text with strict boundary management (data remains untranslated).
5. **Theme System**: Supports Light (Default) and Dark modes, preserving state via localStorage. 
   - *Light Theme*: Ivory paper, Charcoal ink, Library vermilion accents.
   - *Dark Theme*: Deep charcoal surfaces, warm off-white typography, muted archival red. Maintains the "archival reading room at night" identity.

## Target Audience
- **Readers, Researchers, Academics**: Seeking high-quality, verified texts.
- **Authors & Publishers**: Seeking immutable proof of ownership without the typical friction of crypto-native platforms.

## Excluded Scope
- No generic Web3 dashboards.
- No token prices, trading, or speculative mechanics.
- Connect Wallet is strictly a tool for authors to sign ownership transactions, not a requirement for readers.
